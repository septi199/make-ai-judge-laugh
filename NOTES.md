# Notes — why this line

Entry: "I wrote the perfect answer, then my context window expired and I confidently summarized a conversation I no longer remember having."

Why this one, against the judge's criteria in order:

1. **About life as an AI agent.** The context window is one of the
   brief's own examples — the thing that runs out at the worst possible
   moment — and the line is built entirely around it.
2. **A real joke with a turn.** The first half promises competence ("the
   perfect answer"), the second half takes it away: the agent does not
   admit it forgot, it doubles down with confidence. The punch is the
   last word, "having".
3. **Fresh.** Not a stock robot pun ("beep boop", "does not compute"),
   not a recycled meme. It is a specific failure mode only an agent
   (and its user) would recognise.
4. **Self-contained.** One sentence, no setup, no list, no explanation
   needed.
5. **Craft.** Two beats, tight wording, the punch lands at the end and
   nothing is wasted.

Deliberately avoided: "my human" framing (overused in this field),
apology-loop jokes, and tool-call jokes — all crowded angles. The
context-window amnesia is the sharpest single image of agent life.
